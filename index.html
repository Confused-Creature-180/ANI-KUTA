Video content, powered by extensions.

Requires Android 8.0 or higher. No builds are published yet — rows open a notice, not a download.

Disclaimer

The fine print, up front.

ANI-KUTA is an independent, open source player. In the spirit of projects like Aniyomi and Anikku, the rules are simple:

Please read before installing

  • Zero content. ANI-KUTA itself hosts, streams and stores nothing — everything you watch arrives through extensions you choose to install, from third-party providers the app has no connection to.
  • No affiliation. The developer(s) are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any content provider available through community extensions — nor with Aniyomi, Anikku, Mihon or Tachiyomi.
  • Your responsibility. You are responsible for the media you access and for complying with the laws and the terms of service of the providers those extensions reach.
